What VaultCam is

VaultCam is an offline recorder for Android 8.0 and later. It records audio, video, and the screen, encrypts each recording with AES-256-GCM as it is saved, and keeps it in an encrypted vault on your phone. It includes a player, trim and compress tools, export and share, and evidence tools (a SHA-256 fingerprint, a session log, and a chain-of-custody PDF). The App has no internet permission and requires no account.
