OptiVest

Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026  ·  Effective: June 2026

1. Overview

OptiVest is a private investment ledger app for small, trusted groups. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

By using OptiVest, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this policy.

2. Information We Collect

Account information: When you register, we collect your name, email address or phone number, and a hashed version of your password.

Investment data: Project names, transaction amounts, member roles, task details, and uploaded documents that you voluntarily enter into the app.

Device information: Basic device identifiers and app version for crash reporting and support purposes only.

We do not collect: Payment card numbers, bank account details, government IDs, or any sensitive financial credentials.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide, operate, and improve the OptiVest service.
  • To send transactional emails (OTP codes, invitation links, password resets).
  • To enable collaboration features such as shared project access and notifications.
  • To respond to support requests.
  • We do not use your data for advertising or sell it to third parties.

4. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored on Cloudflare's global edge infrastructure and a Neon (PostgreSQL) database hosted in the Asia-Pacific region. All data in transit is encrypted via HTTPS/TLS. Passwords are stored as salted hashes — we cannot recover your plain-text password.

Files and documents you upload are stored in Cloudflare R2 object storage with private access controls.

5. Third-Party Services

  • Resend — transactional email delivery. Subject to Resend's privacy policy.
  • Cloudflare — hosting, edge compute, and file storage. Subject to Cloudflare's privacy policy.
  • Neon — PostgreSQL database hosting. Subject to Neon's privacy policy.
  • Google Play Billing — for premium subscriptions on Android. Subject to Google's privacy policy.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may disclose information only if required by law or to protect the rights and safety of our users.

Within a club or project, members you invite can see the data you share with that group (project records, transactions, tasks). You control who you invite.

7. Data Retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we will delete your personal data within 30 days, except where retention is required by law. Investment records shared with a group may be retained by that group's admin.

8. Your Rights

  • Access: You can request a copy of your personal data at any time.
  • Correction: You can update your profile information in-app.
  • Deletion: You can request deletion of your account and associated data.
  • Portability: You can export your data in JSON format (contact support).

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at support@optivest.app.

9. Children's Privacy

OptiVest is not intended for users under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors. If you believe a minor has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make significant changes, we will notify you in-app. The updated policy will always be available at this URL. Continued use of OptiVest after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
