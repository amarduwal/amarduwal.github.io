Privacy Policy for NudgeBuzz

Last Updated: 20 March 2026

1. Introduction

NudgeBuzz ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

  • Account Information: Email address and display name when you create an account (optional)
  • User Content: Channels, notifications, text, images, videos, and links you create within the app

2.2 Automatically Collected Information

  • Device Information: Device type, operating system version, unique device identifiers
  • Usage Data: App features used, notification delivery statistics (stored locally)
  • Local Storage: All data is stored locally on your device by default

2.3 Optional Cloud Sync (Premium Feature)

If you choose to enable cloud sync, your channels and content are backed up to Firebase Cloud Firestore. This is optional and requires your explicit consent.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain the NudgeBuzz service
  • To send you notifications you've scheduled
  • To sync your data across devices (if you enable cloud sync)
  • To improve app functionality and user experience
  • To provide customer support

4. Data Storage and Security

  • Local Storage: All data is stored locally on your device using AsyncStorage
  • Cloud Storage: If enabled, data is encrypted in transit and stored in Firebase Firestore (Google Cloud)
  • No Analytics: We do not use third-party analytics or tracking services
  • No Ads: We do not display advertisements

5. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do NOT sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties, except:

  • Service Providers: Firebase (Google) for authentication and cloud sync (only if you enable it)
  • Legal Requirements: If required by law or to protect our legal rights

6. Your Rights and Choices

  • Account Deletion: You can delete your account and all associated data at any time in Settings
  • Data Export: You can export all your data in JSON format
  • Opt-out of Cloud Sync: Cloud sync is optional and can be disabled anytime
  • Notification Control: You control all notifications through the app settings

7. Children's Privacy

NudgeBuzz is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Third-Party Services

NudgeBuzz uses the following third-party services:

  • Firebase Authentication: For user login (optional) - Privacy Policy
  • Firebase Firestore: For cloud sync (optional) - Privacy Policy
  • RevenueCat: For premium subscriptions (when available) - Privacy Policy

9. Data Retention

  • Local Data: Stored on your device until you delete it
  • Cloud Data: Stored until you delete your account or disable cloud sync
  • Account Data: Deleted within 30 days of account deletion request

10. International Data Transfers

If you use cloud sync, your data may be transferred to and stored on servers in countries other than your own. We use Google Firebase, which complies with international data protection regulations including GDPR.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.
