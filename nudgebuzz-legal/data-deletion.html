How to Delete Your NudgeBuzz Data

Option 1: Delete from Within the App

Step 1: Open NudgeBuzz app
Step 2: Go to Settings (bottom navigation)
Step 3: Scroll to Data & Backup section
Step 4: Tap "Clear All Data"
Step 5: Confirm deletion
⚠️ Warning: This will permanently delete all channels, content, and settings. This action cannot be undone.

Option 2: Delete Your Account (If Using Cloud Sync)

Step 1: Open NudgeBuzz app
Step 2: Go to Settings
Step 3: Scroll to Cloud Sync section
Step 4: Tap "Sign Out" (this deletes cloud data)
Step 5: Follow Option 1 to delete local data

Option 3: Request Manual Deletion

If you cannot access the app, contact us to manually delete your data:

  • Email: nudgebuzz1@gmail.com
  • Subject: "Data Deletion Request"
  • Include: Your registered email address

We will process your request within 30 days.

What Gets Deleted?

  • ✅ All channels and content you created
  • ✅ Notification schedules
  • ✅ App settings and preferences
  • ✅ Account information (if using cloud sync)
  • ✅ Cloud backup data (if enabled)

What Does NOT Get Deleted?

  • ❌ Notifications already delivered to your device (managed by Android system)
  • ❌ Anonymous usage logs (if any, retained for 90 days for troubleshooting)

After Deletion

  • Local data is deleted immediately
  • Cloud data is deleted within 30 days
  • You can reinstall and start fresh anytime
  • Premium subscriptions must be cancelled separately via Google Play
💡 Tip: Before deleting, consider exporting your data via Settings → Export Data if you want to keep a backup.
