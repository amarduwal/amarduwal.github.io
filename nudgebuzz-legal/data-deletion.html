🗑️ Data

How to Delete Your Data

You have full control over your NudgeBuzz data. Follow any of the options below to delete it.

Option 1 — Delete from the App

The fastest way to remove all local data:

1
Open the NudgeBuzz app
2
Tap Settings in the bottom navigation
3
Scroll to Data & Backup
4
Tap "Clear All Data"
5
Confirm the deletion
⚠️ Warning: This permanently deletes all channels, content, schedules, and settings. This action cannot be undone.

Option 2 — Delete Your Account (Cloud Sync Users)

If you enabled cloud sync, follow these steps to remove your cloud data first:

1
Open the NudgeBuzz app
2
Go to Settings
3
Scroll to Cloud Sync and tap "Sign Out" — this schedules cloud data for deletion
4
Then follow Option 1 to delete local data

Option 3 — Request Manual Deletion

If you cannot access the app, contact us to delete your data manually:

✉
Email nudgebuzz1@gmail.com with subject "Data Deletion Request" and your registered email address

We will process your request within 30 days.

What Gets Deleted

  • All channels and content you created
  • Notification schedules
  • App settings and preferences
  • Account information (if using cloud sync)
  • Cloud backup data (if enabled)

What Does Not Get Deleted

  • Notifications already delivered to your device (managed by Android system)
  • Anonymous usage logs, if any — retained for up to 90 days for troubleshooting

After Deletion

  • Local data is removed immediately
  • Cloud data is deleted within 30 days
  • You can reinstall and start fresh at any time
💡 Tip: Before deleting, consider exporting your data via Settings → Export Data if you'd like to keep a backup.
