Support VaultCam

VaultCam is free, with no ads, no paywalls, and no account. If it has been useful to you and you want to help keep it that way, you can send a small gift below. It is completely optional.

Before you give

  • A donation is a voluntary gift, not a purchase. It does not add features, remove limits, or buy priority support. Every VaultCam feature is free for everyone.
  • Donations are non-refundable. Please send only what you are comfortable giving.
  • Crypto transfers cannot be reversed. Check the address and the network in your wallet before you send. Funds sent to the wrong address or network are lost.
  • Each payment is handled by the service you use (Khalti, eSewa, PayPal, or a crypto network), under that service's own terms and privacy policy.
