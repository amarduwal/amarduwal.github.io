Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 2, 2026

In short: Shadebloom has no accounts and never asks for your name, email or anything you type. Your game progress stays on your phone. The game is free and shows ads from Google AdMob, and it uses Google Firebase to count how levels are played and to report crashes. Where the law requires it, the game asks for your consent before ads load, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings.

Who we are

Shadebloom is made by Amar Duwal, an individual developer in Nepal ("we", "us"). Questions about this policy or your data: duwal.amar2006@gmail.com.

What the app does not collect

The app has no sign-in and no accounts. Our own code does not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, precise location, photos, or any text you enter. We do not run our own servers, so we do not receive or store data about you.

Data stored on your phone

The app saves your game progress and settings on your device: which levels you solved, hints left, sound and vibration choices, and ad timing counters. Android Auto Backup may copy this save to your Google account, encrypted by Android, so it can return when you set up a new phone.

To delete it, uninstall the app, or open Android Settings > Apps > Shadebloom > Storage > Clear storage.

Services from Google

The app uses the following Google services. Each one collects the data listed while you use the app and processes it under Google's Privacy Policy.

ServiceWhat it collectsWhy
Google AdMob Advertising ID, IP address, device information (model, Android version, language), and ad interactions such as views and taps To show ads, measure them, and prevent ad fraud. See how Google uses data from apps that use its services.
Google User Messaging Platform Your consent choice, stored on your device and sent with ad requests To ask for and respect your consent where the law requires it.
Firebase Analytics A random app instance ID, game events such as a level started or solved and hints used, device model, Android version, and approximate country derived from your IP address To learn which levels are too hard or too easy and improve the game. Kept for 2 months.
Firebase Crashlytics Crash reports with technical details of the error, device state and a random installation ID To find and fix crashes. Kept for 90 days.
Firebase Remote Config A random installation ID To download game settings, such as how often ads may appear, without an app update.
Google Play In-App Review Handled by Google Play To ask once whether you would like to rate the game.

None of these identifiers is your name or email address. Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

Ads and your choices

  • When ads appear: a full-screen ad may appear only after you solve a level and tap Next, never while you play and never in the first 10 levels. Rewarded ads play only when you choose them, to get a hint or 30 minutes without full-screen ads.
  • Consent: if you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, or in a US state with a privacy law, the game shows Google's consent message before ads load. Without your consent, ads may be limited or not personalized.
  • Change your choice: open the game's Settings (gear icon) and tap the shield icon, Privacy options. This option appears wherever it is required.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete it in Android Settings > Privacy > Ads (the exact path depends on your phone).

Legal bases (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

We rely on your consent for personalized ads, collected through Google's consent message. We rely on our legitimate interests in keeping the game working and improving it for crash reports and basic, pseudonymous usage statistics, and for showing non-personalized ads that keep the game free.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your data, to object to or restrict its use, to withdraw consent at any time (Privacy options in Settings), and to complain to your data protection authority. Because we do not hold data on our own servers, most requests are fulfilled through the tools above: Clear storage, Privacy options, and your Android advertising settings. You can also email us and we will help.

US state privacy laws

We do not sell your personal information for money. Showing personalized ads through Google AdMob may count as "sharing" or "targeted advertising" under some US state laws. You can opt out in the game's Privacy options or by deleting your advertising ID.

Children

Shadebloom is not directed at children under 13 and is listed on Google Play for ages 13 and up. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has used the app, email us and we will help remove any data we can.

International transfers

Google processes data on servers around the world, including in the United States, under its own safeguards for international transfers.

Retention

Game progress stays on your device until you clear it or uninstall the app. Firebase Analytics data is kept for 2 months and Crashlytics reports for 90 days. Ad data is kept by Google according to its policies.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will post the new version on this page with a new effective date. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's Google Play listing.
