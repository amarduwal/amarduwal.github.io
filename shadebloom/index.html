Shape the shade. Let the garden bloom.

Shadebloom is a logic puzzle of light and shade. Raise stone pillars so their shadows give every plant exactly the sun or shade it needs. Every level has one solution, and you can always find it by reasoning, never by guessing.

No accounts, no timers, no lives. Works in any language.

How to play

Three ideas carry all 99 levels.

Raise a pillar

Tap a pillar to raise it from 0 to 3 blocks. Its shadow is as long as the pillar is tall and falls away from the sun.

Read the plants

Sunflowers want sun. Ferns want shade. Hydrangeas want sun in the morning and shade later in the day.

Watch the day pass

Later levels show the garden at two or three times of day, and every plant must be happy at each one.

Made to be fair and calm

One answer, no guessing

Each level is checked by a solver to have exactly one solution that logic alone can reach.

Play at your pace

No timers, lives or energy. Undo any move, reset a level, or use a hint when you are stuck.

Perfect solves

Solve a level in the fewest taps without a hint to earn a Perfect and grow an extra leaf in your garden.
