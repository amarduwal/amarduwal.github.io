Questions

How do I play?

Tap a stone pillar to raise it one step, from 0 to 3; one more tap brings it back to 0. A pillar casts a shadow as long as it is tall, away from the sun. Sunflowers need sun, ferns need shade, and hydrangeas need sun at the first time of day and shade at the last. When every plant is happy, the level is solved. Some levels have more than one time of day: switch between them with the sun buttons at the top and make every plant happy at each.

What does the number in the corner mean on later levels?

From the Terraces chapter on, levels have a limited number of blocks. The counter shows how many are left; the total height of all pillars must fit within it.

Is every level solvable without guessing?

Yes. Each level has exactly one solution, and every step can be worked out by logic.

How do hints work?

The lightbulb sets one pillar to its correct height and locks it. You start with 3 hints and get one more for each chapter you complete. When you have none left, you can watch a short ad to get one. A level solved with a hint does not earn a Perfect.

What is a Perfect?

Solving a level in the fewest possible taps, without a hint. Perfect levels grow an extra leaf in the garden.

When do ads appear?

Never while you play. A full-screen ad may appear after you solve a level and tap Next, starting from level 11 and spaced out over time. Rewarded ads only play when you choose them.

What is quiet time?

Tap the moon button and watch one ad to play for 30 minutes without full-screen ads. The button shows the minutes left, and quiet time continues if you close and reopen the game.

How is my progress saved? Can I move it to a new phone?

Progress is saved on your phone automatically after every move. If Android backup is on in your phone's settings, it is backed up to your Google account and comes back when you install the game on a new phone with the same account.

How do I change my privacy or ad choices?

Open Settings (gear icon) in the game and tap the shield icon, Privacy options. It appears where privacy laws require it. You can also reset your advertising ID in Android settings. See the Privacy Policy.

How do I start over?

Open Android Settings > Apps > Shadebloom > Storage > Clear storage. This deletes all progress on that phone and cannot be undone.

The game has no words. Is that on purpose?

Yes. Everything is shown with icons and numbers so the game works in any language. Screen readers (TalkBack) read a description of every button and board item.
