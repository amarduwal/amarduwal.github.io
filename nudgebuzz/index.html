FAQ

Questions people actually ask

Is NudgeBuzz really free?

Yes — every feature is available to everyone, with no subscriptions or locked tiers. Banner and native ads keep it free to run.

Does it work without an internet connection?

Yes. Channels, scheduling, alarms, Timer & Stopwatch, and Location Trigger all run entirely on your device. Only AI content generation and Groups & Nudges need a connection.

Do I need to create an account?

No. NudgeBuzz works fully anonymously with local storage. An account is only needed for Groups & Nudges.

Can I make to-do lists, not just reminders?

Yes — To-Do Lists are checklists with due-date reminders per item, subtasks, streaks, and natural-language quick-add ("tomorrow at 5pm"), plus the same Alarm Mode, Location Trigger, and AI generation as every other channel.

How does Location Trigger use my location?

It's evaluated entirely on-device to detect arrival at a place you've chosen. Your location is never sent to a server — see the Privacy Policy for details.

What languages does AI content support?

12 languages, including English, Spanish, French, Hindi, Nepali, Arabic, Chinese, and Japanese — generated natively, not translated afterward.

Can I get my data out if I stop using the app?

Yes, anytime — Settings → Export Data saves everything as a real file you can restore later or move to another device.
