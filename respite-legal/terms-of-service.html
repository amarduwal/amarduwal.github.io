Description of Service

Respite is a mobile application offering breathing exercises, a panic attack timer, grounding techniques, a sensory reset kit, mood tracking, sleep cycle calculation, CBT thought-record cards, a habit tracker, hydration logging, and a gratitude journal. The app works fully offline and requires no account.
