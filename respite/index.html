FAQ

Questions people actually ask

Is Respite really free?▼

Yes. Every crisis tool and every daily tool is free forever. Respite+ is an optional one-time unlock for small delights (an AMOLED theme, extra breathing pattern slots); it never gates anything that helps in a hard moment.

Does it need an internet connection?▼

No. Every tool works fully offline. The only network use anywhere in the app is the optional Respite+ purchase, handled entirely by Google Play Billing.

Do I need to create an account?▼

No. There's no login anywhere in the app, and there never will be.

What happens to my data?▼

It stays on your device, in a local database, until you export or delete it yourself. See the Privacy Policy for the complete picture.

Can I get my data out?▼

Yes, anytime. Settings → Export & Data lets you export any tool as CSV, everything as one JSON backup, or a one-page printable summary to bring to a therapist.

Is Respite a substitute for therapy?▼

No. It's a self-help toolkit, not a medical device or emergency service. If you're in crisis or in danger, contact your local emergency number or a crisis hotline.
